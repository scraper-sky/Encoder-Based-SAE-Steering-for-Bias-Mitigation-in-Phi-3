\subsection{Utility (perplexity)}

We measure WikiText-2 (raw) perplexity on a 50k-token sliding-window subset to assess language modeling utility. The baseline model achieves a perplexity of $5.697$, while encoder-direction SAE steering at $\alpha{=}2.0$ increases this to $6.351$, representing an $11.5\%$ relative increase. This modest utility degradation is expected given the bias reduction achieved, and demonstrates that encoder-based steering provides a favorable tradeoff between bias mitigation and model utility.
